01 / THE WORKSHOP

From pre-authored worlds
to human-aware experiences.

What if an immersive environment could respond to how you feel, where your attention goes, and what you need next?

SENSE-XR brings together researchers in physiological computing, extended reality, human–computer interaction, and generative AI to explore that question. We study how human signals can inform environments, narratives, dialogue, virtual characters, and interactions in real time.

Our focus is the whole feedback loop: sensing a person’s state, understanding it, adapting or generating an experience, and evaluating the result. Contributions to any one part of this loop are welcome.

02 / RESEARCH DIRECTIONS

Many signals.
One shared conversation.

We welcome rigorous components, integrated systems, early ideas, and working demonstrations.

01

Sensing & modelling

Physiological, neural, gaze, speech, facial, motion, and interaction signals. Multimodal fusion and models of affect, workload, attention, engagement, fatigue, and intent.

EEG / EDA / HRV / GAZE / BCI
02

Adaptation & generation

Adaptive and neuroadaptive XR. Generative environments, narratives, virtual humans, dialogue, audio, video, and 3D content. LLM/VLM agents and embodied prompting.

GENERATIVE AI / AGENTS / PERSONALIZATION
03

Systems & evaluation

Closed-loop architectures, latency, stability, datasets, and benchmarks. Presence, quality of experience, longitudinal personalization, privacy, safety, and user agency.

REAL-TIME SYSTEMS / QoE / RESPONSIBLE XR
04

Applications & experiences

Training, education, healthcare, rehabilitation, games, immersive storytelling, social XR, collaborative work, and industry prototypes.

Submission link coming soon

Submissions will be managed through the AIxVR EasyChair account. The workshop submission link will be published here when available.

Review process & publication

Each submission will be reviewed by at least two independent experts for relevance, originality, technical quality, clarity, and potential to stimulate discussion. A third review will be sought when scores diverge.

Decisions will be made collectively by the organizers, with conflicts handled by non-conflicted chairs and reviewers. Organizer-authored papers will follow the same review criteria.

Accepted papers meeting conference and IEEE requirements will be included in the AIxVR 2027 proceedings and submitted for inclusion in IEEE Xplore.
